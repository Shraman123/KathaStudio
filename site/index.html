Story → culturally adapted → voiced audio drama

Adaptation, not translation.

Give Katha an English story. It rewrites the names, places, food, festivals and idioms so the story feels native to a Bengali listener, keeps every plot beat exactly, turns it into an audio-drama script, and performs it with a designed voice for each character on Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS.

Same beats, new world

A Vermont pie contest becomes a Poush Mela payesh rivalry.

The source is an original short story set in small-town Vermont. Katha chose every substitution below itself and logged a reason for each. The grief, the rival, the lost ingredient and the ending are untouched.

SourceEnglish · Vermont

Every November, the town of Maple Hollow, Vermont, held its Harvest Fair on the village green, and every November for thirty-one years, Martha Quinn's apple pie won the blue ribbon.

This year, Martha was not sure she would bake at all. Her husband, Walt, had died in March. It was Walt who peeled the apples in one long, unbroken curl, and Walt who hummed off-key while the crust browned.

AdaptedBengali, West Bengal

Every November, the town of Shantiniketan held its annual Poush Mela on the village grounds, and for thirty-one years, Protima-di’s Nolen Gurer Payesh had won the first prize.

This year, Protima was not sure she would cook at all. Her husband, Dadu, had passed away in March. It was Dadu who would carefully grate the fresh date-palm jaggery into the simmering milk, and Dadu who would hum Tagore songs off-key while the rice softened.

Pipeline

Seven stages and a studio. Each stage reruns on its own.

Every stage writes its output to disk. You can inspect the change log before any audio is made, edit a line in the script and re-render only that chunk, or stop halfway and pick up tomorrow.

Ingest

Splits on chapters, then paragraphs, to fit the model's context. A story bible keeps names consistent from the first chunk to the last.

Adapt

Rewrites for the target culture. Foreign elements central to the plot must get a native equivalent with the same function. Every change is logged.

Script

Scenes of {speaker, text, style, tags}. Spoken text is verbatim; stage directions are rejected. Every plot beat must be covered.

Cast

A designed voice per character from its description, with previews. Voice ids are cached and never recreated.

Render

Plans requests around API rules: designed voices one turn at a time, prebuilt voices two speakers per request.

Stitch

300 ms between lines, 1 s between scenes, each chunk matched to the same loudness, −1 dBFS ceiling. Exports MP3 + WAV.

Report

What changed and why, the cast, durations, API calls, cost at paid rates, and whether it fits the free tier.

Studio

A Gradio UI for the whole flow: upload, pick culture and language, preview voices, render, play, download.

Engineering

Built against the real API, not the brochure.

Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS shipped in September 2026. Every request shape was checked against the docs and then against live calls; what we learned is written up in API_NOTES.md.

Self-repairing structured output

Adaptations and scripts are pydantic-validated. When the model breaks a rule, its own errors go back to it once for a fix.

Quota-aware by design

The free tier allows about 10 TTS requests a day. Daily-quota errors stop cleanly instead of retrying pointlessly, and the render resumes tomorrow.

Resumable, fingerprinted chunks

Each audio chunk is fingerprinted by its text, style and voice. Change one line and only that chunk is re-rendered.

Swappable writer

Gemini 3.8 Flash by default (free tier), with an automatic fallback model. Claude is one flag away: --llm claude.

Safety-respecting fallbacks

Google refuses to design child voices. Katha gives those characters a library voice and never prompts around the policy.

Honest cost reports

Prices live in config, not code. Every episode's report shows paid-rate cost and whether the run fits the free tier.

Quickstart

Your story, in five commands.

# Python 3.11+, a Gemini API key in .env
git clone https://github.com/Shraman123/KathaStudio && cd KathaStudio
pip install -e ".[ui]"
katha all samples/the_last_pie.md --culture "Bengali, West Bengal" --lang en --dry-run
katha all samples/the_last_pie.md --culture "Bengali, West Bengal" --lang en
katha ui          # or use the studio in your browser

Narrate in your own voice, and only your own. Katha can replicate a narrator voice from a 10–30 s clip plus a recorded consent statement from the same speaker. Only replicate your own voice or one whose owner has given explicit, recorded consent.
